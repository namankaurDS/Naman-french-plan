3-Month Learning Phases

Each phase builds on the last. Check the Weekly Plan tab for exactly what to do each day — no guessing.

Phase 1 · Month 1
Foundation
15 Mar → 15 Apr · A1 → A2
Build complete sentence patterns. Greetings, introductions, daily routines, simple admin phrases. Learn everything as full sentences — never isolated words. Start slow, get the rhythm right.
Easy French + Lingoni + Alice Ayel
Phase 2 · Month 2
Conversation Bridge
15 Apr → 15 May · A2 → B1
Real dialogue practice. Phone calls, meetings, emails, expressing opinions. Begin shadowing native speakers. Remove English subtitles. Focus on listening at natural speed.
InnerFrench + Pierre + Géraldine
Phase 3 · Month 3
Fluency Push
15 May → 15 Jun · B1 → B2
Sustain longer conversations. Complex admin, formal emails, handling misunderstandings. Watch storytelling channels without subtitles. Write full professional emails in French.
Cyprien + Bruno Maltor + InnerFrench
⚡ Key insight: Since speaking & listening feel harder — in Phases 2 and 3, move 10 min from reading into extra shadowing. Reading is your strong skill — use that advantage to push the harder skills forward.

Language Milestones

Real things you can DO in French — not exam theory. Tick each one only when you feel genuinely confident. This is your true progress map.

💡 These are based on what you can actually produce and understand, not what you've studied. Your overall level matches whichever column is mostly ticked. Don't rush — wait until you're sure.
👂
Listening Channels
Natural speech · Real conversations · Gradual speed increase
🎧 Strategy: Never listen passively. Pause after each sentence and repeat it before moving on. Subtitles ON in Phase 1, French-only in Phase 2, none in Phase 3.
🗣️
Speaking Channels
Shadowing · Pronunciation · Situational dialogues
🎤 Shadowing rule: After every listening session, pick 5 sentences and repeat them immediately — same rhythm, speed and intonation. This is the fastest way to build speaking confidence.
📖
Reading Channels
Transcripts · Dialogues · Your strongest skill — use it to reinforce the others
📖 Strategy: Always read aloud. Use video transcripts from your listening channels — reading what you just heard doubles the impact. Note 3 full sentences (not words!) to reuse.
✍️
Writing Channels & Method
Professional French · Emails · Admin · Sentence swap technique
✍️ Daily starters: "Aujourd'hui, j'ai... / Je dois... / J'aimerais..." — Use these 3 starters every day. By Phase 3, write a full professional email in French.
✨
Storytelling Channels
Weekly experiences · Event stories · Mini-narratives — the most natural way to absorb sentence flow
✨ Why storytelling works: Stories force your brain to predict what comes next. This is how you stop translating word-by-word and start understanding French as one continuous flow. One good story video can fuel your entire 2-hour session across all 4 skills.
